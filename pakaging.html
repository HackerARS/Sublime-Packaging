FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our packaging services, ordering process, and more.

What is the minimum order quantity?
Our minimum order quantity starts from just 50 units for most products. We cater to both small businesses and large enterprises with flexible order quantities. For custom rigid boxes and luxury packaging, minimum quantities may vary — contact us for specific details.
How long does production take?
Standard production takes 7-10 business days after design approval. Rush orders can be completed in 3-5 business days with an expedited fee. Complex projects like rigid boxes with special finishes may take 10-15 days.
Do you offer free design services?
Yes! We provide complimentary design consultation and free 3D mockups for all orders. Our professional design team will work with you to create the perfect packaging that reflects your brand identity.
What materials do you use?
We use premium materials including Kraft, Cardboard, Rigid Board, Corrugated, Duplex, Art Card, and Recycled Paper. All materials are sourced from trusted suppliers and meet international quality standards.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we offer worldwide shipping! We deliver across Pakistan and internationally to USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Australia, and other countries. Shipping costs and timelines vary based on destination.
Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?
Absolutely! We offer sample production so you can review the quality, material, printing, and finishing before committing to a full order. Sample costs can be adjusted against your bulk order payment.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, online payments, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and cash on delivery (for Lahore orders). For international clients, we accept wire transfers and PayPal. A 50% advance is required to initiate production.
Do you offer eco-friendly packaging options?
Yes! We offer recyclable kraft boxes, biodegradable packaging, recycled paper options, and soy-based inks. Our eco-friendly line is perfect for brands that prioritize environmental responsibility.
